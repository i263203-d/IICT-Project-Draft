GILGIT-BALTISTAN • ELEVATION 2,500M

Hunza & Nagar Valley

The crown jewel of the Karakoram Highway. Ancient Silk Road forts, sapphire glacial waters of Attabad Lake, dramatic Passu cathedral spires, and welcoming Wakhi hospitality.

The Shangri-La of the Karakoram

Hunza is one of the most culturally rich and visually dramatic mountain valleys on Earth. Framed by iconic 7,000-meter giants—including Rakaposhi (7,788m), Ladyfinger Peak, and Ultar Sar—it offers an extraordinary blend of historical heritage, apricot orchards, and high-altitude exploration.

Altit & Baltit Forts

900-year-old royal palaces overlooking Karimabad with preserved Tibetan-influenced woodwork and ancient defense watchtowers.

Attabad Lake

Formed in 2010, this 21km turquoise glacial lake offers speedboating and luxury waterfront stays at Luxus Hunza.

Passu Cones & Suspension Bridge

Also called the Cathedral Peaks. Walk the nerve-wracking Passu suspension bridge over the roaring Hunza River.

Khunjerab Pass (4,693m)

The world's highest paved international border crossing and protected sanctuary for Himalayan ibex and snow leopards.

Seasonal Highlights

Spring (April): The valley erupts into white and pink cherry/apricot blossoms against snow peaks.
Summer (June–August): Warm sunny days, lush green barley fields, and high passes fully open.
Autumn (October): Golden hour foliage galore with ablaze yellow poplars and orange apricot trees.
