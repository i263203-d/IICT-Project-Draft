BALTISTAN REGION • ELEVATION 4,114M

Skardu & Deosai Plains

The gateway to K2 and the Throne Room of the Mountain Gods. Discover Earth's second-highest plateau, the mystical Katpana Cold Desert, and 400-year-old royal fort palaces.

The Land of Giants Experience

Deosai National Park spans over 3,000 square kilometers at an average elevation of 4,114 meters (13,497 feet). In the summer months of July and August, this windswept wilderness bursts into a surreal tapestry of millions of alpine wildflowers, crisscrossed by crystal trout streams and roamed by the protected Himalayan Brown Bear.

Sheosar Lake

A heart-shaped alpine lake situated at 4,142m, reflecting the snow-covered south face of Nanga Parbat in the distance.

Katpana Cold Desert

Dramatic high-altitude sand dunes nestled at 2,228 meters that get covered in snow during winter.

Shigar & Khaplu Forts

400-year-old royal Raja palaces restored by the Aga Khan Cultural Service into luxury Serena heritage hotels.

Shangrila Lake Resort

The famous Lower Kachura Lake with its red-roofed pagoda cottages and DC-3 aircraft cabin café.

Altitude & Acclimatization Guidelines

Because Deosai sits over 4,000 meters above sea level, DEOSAI Expeditions itineraries always include 2 nights of gradual acclimatization in Skardu town (2,228m) before crossing the high plateau. All our dedicated 4x4 vehicles are equipped with portable oxygen cylinders, pulse oximeters, and satellite phones.
