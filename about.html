// THE DEOSAI HERITAGE

Born in the High Karakoram

We are not third-party city brokers. We are mountain natives, seasoned expedition logisticians, and high-altitude custodians dedicated to revealing Pakistan's grandest frontiers safely.

// OUR PHILOSOPHY

Reimagining Travel in Northern Pakistan

For decades, travelers venturing into Gilgit-Baltistan had to endure unreliable vehicles, sub-standard accommodations, and opaque pricing. DEOSAI Expeditions was founded in Islamabad and Skardu to set a new benchmark of excellence.

We operate our own private fleet of high-clearance 4x4 Toyota Land Cruisers, employ certified high-altitude Balti and Hunzai mountaineering guides, and maintain strict medical protocols including portable oxygen kits and satellite communicators on every remote crossing.

// THE 4 CORNERSTONES

What Sets DEOSAI Apart

Native Mountain Guides

Our lead guides are born and raised in Hunza, Nagar, and Baltistan. They know every crevasse, weather sign, and local legend.

Private 4x4 Fleet

No outsourced third-party vans. We operate meticulously maintained Toyota Land Cruiser Prados and custom-suspension HiAces.

Leave No Trace

We strictly follow eco-tourism guidelines. All non-biodegradable waste is packed out of Deosai and the Baltoro glacier.

Transparent Pricing

No surprise fuel surcharges or hidden toll costs. What we quote in our custom plan is exactly what you invest.
