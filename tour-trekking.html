Expedition Profile & Itinerary

Often described by mountaineers as the greatest mountain trek on planet Earth, the Baltoro Glacier corridor takes you directly beneath four 8,000-meter peaks (K2, Gasherbrum I, Broad Peak, and Gasherbrum II). Our expedition is staffed by certified Balti high-altitude guides and equipped with medical hyperbaric bags and satellite emergency communications.

Day-by-Day Trek Itinerary

Meet team at Islamabad airport, expedition briefing at the Department of Tourist Services, followed by the world-famous mountain flight to Skardu soaring past Nanga Parbat (8,126m). Acclimatization walk around Skardu bazaar and Shangrila Lake.

Drive through Shigar valley along rugged gorges to Askole, the last permanently inhabited village of Baltistan. The foot expedition begins. Hike along the roaring Braldu river to Jhola campsite (approx. 6 hours hiking).

First dramatic views of the Trango Towers and Cathedral Spires. Vital acclimatization rest day at Paiju, followed by stepping onto the massive moraine of the 63km-long Baltoro Glacier. Camp at Urdukas on a scenic terrace directly facing the sheer granite walls of Great Trango.

Trek surrounded by Masherbrum (7,821m) and Gasherbrum IV. Reach Concordia, where the Baltoro and Godwin-Austen glaciers collide. Unobstructed 360-degree amphitheater views of K2, Broad Peak, and Mitre Peak.

Full day excursion across Godwin-Austen glacier to Broad Peak Base Camp and K2 Base Camp. Pay solemn respects at the historic Art Gilkey Memorial honoring fallen climbers on K2's Abruzzi Spur. Return to Concordia.

Retrace steps down the glacier via Goro I and Paiju to Askole. 4×4 return transfer to Skardu. Expedition celebration dinner and scenic return flight to Islamabad.
