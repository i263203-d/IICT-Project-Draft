THE GEOGRAPHIC REALMS

Valleys of the North

Northern Pakistan is where the three greatest mountain ranges on Earth collide: the Karakoram, the Himalayas, and the Hindu Kush. Choose your frontier below.

Hunza • 2,500m
Best: Apr – Oct Avg Stay: 7–9 Days

Hunza & Nagar Valley

The legend of Shangri-La. Ancient Silk Road forts of Altit and Baltit, the turquoise expanse of Attabad Lake, the dramatic Passu Cones, and the 4,693m China border crossing at Khunjerab Pass.

Paved KKH Highway Explore Valley Guide →
Baltistan • 4,114m
Best: Jun – Sep (Bloom) Avg Stay: 8–10 Days

Skardu & Deosai Plains

The Land of Giants. The world's second-highest alpine plateau, crystal-clear Sheosar Lake, Katpana Cold Desert dunes with snow backgrounds, and the historic Shigar & Khaplu royal forts.

Direct Skardu Flights Explore Plateau Guide →
Diamer • 3,300m
Best: May – Oct Avg Stay: 5–7 Days

Fairy Meadows & Nanga Parbat

In the direct shadow of the Killer Mountain. Boarding the cliffside 4x4 track at Raikot Bridge, hiking through pine forests, and watching the sunset ignite the 8,126m sheer ice face.

Mountain 4x4 Required Explore Meadows Guide →
KPK • 2,000m
Best: Year-Round Avg Stay: 4–6 Days

Swat Valley & Kalam

The Switzerland of the East. Lush alpine meadows, Mahodand Lake trout fishing, Kundol glacial waters, 2,000-year-old Buddhist heritage stupas, and premier winter skiing at Malam Jabba.
