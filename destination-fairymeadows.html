DIAMER REGION • ELEVATION 3,300M

Fairy Meadows & Nanga Parbat

Resting directly beneath the sheer north ice face of Nanga Parbat (8,126m). A pristine alpine sanctuary of pine forests, log cabins, and cliffside jeep tracks.

The Killer Mountain Amphitheater

Fairy Meadows is named by German mountaineers who were stunned by its magical setting. The journey begins at Raikhot Bridge on the Karakoram Highway, where licensed mountain drivers take you up the cliff-hugging jeep track to Tattu village, followed by a scenic 3-hour hike through pine woods.

Raikhot Jeep Track

Considered one of the world's most thrilling mountain jeep rides, piloted exclusively by local drivers.

Stargazing & Log Cabins

Zero light pollution. The Milky Way arches directly over the illuminated ice face of Nanga Parbat.

Beyal Camp (3,500m)

An easy 2-hour day trek through birch woods to a peaceful high camp with glacier views.

Nanga Parbat Base Camp

At 3,967 meters, touching the edge of the Raikhot glacier beneath the historic German memorial.
